# Commit Messages

Keep commit messages concise.

## Structure

- A single **subject line** that states, standalone, *what* changed. No prefixes like "chore", "fix" or "bug". Subject line is always lower-case, unless it's for an acronym like GPIO
- Optionally, a **single paragraph** of body providing more detail on *what* changed
  and *why* it changed.

## Rules

- Do not describe implementation details or design in the message.
- The subject line must stand on its own and convey the what.
- The body, if present, is one paragraph: more on the what, plus the why.
- Prefer no body over a body that merely restates the subject.
- Write in the imperative mood (e.g. "add", "remove", "update").
